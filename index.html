The Order

About Us

Misfits & Rejects is a VRChat community built for those who never found their place. We welcome creators, explorers, gamers, and outsiders who want a place where they can belong.

Our Story

Founded by a handful of wanderers who never fit the mold, Misfits & Rejects grew from a small circle of friends into a full underground family — built inside VRChat, but bound by something older.

Our Values

Respect over judgment. Loyalty over convenience. We protect our own, welcome the different, and never ask anyone to shrink themselves to belong here.

Community Identity

Misfits and Rejects is built around community, freedom, music, and meeting people. We keep things relaxed, but there are still lines that cannot be crossed. Respect the environment and the people around you.

Why We Exist

Because not everyone belongs everywhere — and some of us had to build our own place. This is that place, for anyone still looking for theirs.

Rules of the Order

Community Rules

  • 18+ ONLY - This is an adults only environment. No minors alowed. If staff reasonably suspect someone is underage, they may be removed immediately. We do not debate age verification issues in instance.
  • ZERO TOLERANCE - We do NOT tolorate: -Racism -Sexism -Homophobia or Transphobia -Pedophilia or predatory behavior -Harassment or targeted hate
  • INSTANCE RULES - Respect people; Do not bully, harass, or intentionally make others uncomfortable. -No NSFW content -Keep ERP and explicit sexual content out of public spaces. -No poaching -Do not recruit members away from the community or partnered groups. -No clipping -Do not invade people’s space through avatars or camera exploits. -No age regression content -No excessive personal drama -Staff are here to moderate the club, not mediate personal relationships. -Follow VRChat TOS
  • TROLLING - Light trolling and joking around is fine. The atmosphere is meant to be fun. However, repeated disruption, harassment, griefing, or ruining the experience for others will get staff involved quickly.
  • STAFF DISCRETION - Every situation is different. Staff may act based on the context of a situation to protect the environment and keep things running smoothly.

Staff Rules

  • Remain professional during moderation
  • Avoid escalating conflicts emotionally
  • Communicate clearly with one another
  • Handle incidents consistently
  • Avoid public power trips or humiliation tactics
  • Document severe incidents when necessary
  • Support fellow staff during active moderation situations
  • Staff authority exists to maintain stability, not ego.
  • FINAL NOTE - -These rules are ultimately enforced at staff discretion. Every situation is different, and moderators are trusted to make fair decisions that protect the environment and community culture of Misfits and Rejects.

The Hierarchy

Rank System

From first arrival to founding leadership — the path through the family.

Inittates

Initiates are staff members currently within their trial period. This stage exists to evaluate consistency, attitude, professionalism, and ability to work within the moderation team.

Enforcers

Enforcers are the primary moderation and security force of the group. They handle day to day moderation issues and are responsible for maintaining the atmosphere of the instance.

Shotcallers

Shotcallers are responsible for active instance management and operational leadership during events. They coordinate security rotations and help guide newer staff members into the moderation system.

Overseer

Overseers are senior management staff trusted to solve serious issues independently. They are expected to make level headed decisions under pressure and maintain stability within the group environment.

Exarch

Exarchs are director level staff who operate between ownership and senior management. They are trusted to oversee major departments, guide management decisions, and ensure that the structure of Misfits and Rejects runs smoothly across events, staff, security, and community operations.

Arbiters

Arbiters are the direct right hand staff to the Architects. They are trusted to operate independently and may handle nearly any responsibility an Architect would handle.

The Architects

The Architects are responsible for the overall direction, operation, and long term vision of the group. They oversee all major decisions regarding staffing, partnerships, branding, security structure, and community development.

The Philosopher King

Before there were members, there was an idea. Before there was a community, there was The Philosopher King. As the creator and sole holder of this role, The Philosopher King serves as the ultimate authority over Misfits and Rejects, preserving its identity, shaping its future, and ensuring that every decision reflects the principles upon which it was built. Every partnership, every event, every leader, and every evolution of the club exists because of the foundation laid by the Founder.
